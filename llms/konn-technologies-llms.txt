## Products

- [DesignFit](https://konnhomes.com/designfit): 300 JOD, 10-day land assessment - 2D layouts, site plan, budget and timeline.
- [ArchDesign](https://konnhomes.com/archdesign): 4,000 JOD 3D design with a locked-in construction price, deducted from construction.
- [Konnify](https://konnhomes.com/konnify): Adapts a design commissioned from an outside architect onto the Konn engineering system.
- [Design Studio](https://design.konn.tech): Browse Konn home designs by floor type, starting size, and style, with floor plans and pricing.
- [Design Match](https://match.konnhomes.com): Recommends a Konn home design from stated bedrooms, size, and spaces.
- [Specifications](https://konnhomes.com/specifications): Reinforced concrete structure, 10+ cm wall insulation, sealed envelope; Origin and Prime finish tiers.
